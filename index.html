    How to play

    Controls

    • W A S D or ↑↓←→ — drive
    • Space — fire your power-up
    • V — switch camera (top-down → chase → first-person)
    • M — mute / unmute
    • Mouse drag — orbit the camera (right-click resets)
    • Esc — pause / leave
    • Touch — drag the joystick (mobile)

    The job

    • Vacuum every speck of dirt to unlock the next door.
    • Five rooms, one final dock. Beat them all.
    • Pets patrol every room. Touching one stuns you for two seconds and costs points.
    • Mines and bombs you drop are dangerous to everyone — including you. Don't dawdle nearby.
    • Co-op: shared score. Versus: every pilot for themselves.

    Power-ups

    • ⚡ Turbo — 1.5× speed for 5s
    • 🔫 Turret — auto-fires for 8s
    • 💣 Mine — drops behind, blasts pets
    • 💥 Big bomb — wide blast radius
    • 🤫 Stealth — pets ignore you for 4s
